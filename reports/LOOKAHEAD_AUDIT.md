# Anti-Lookahead & Temporal Causality Audit Report

**Audit Target**: Signal Generation & Execution Timing Engine  
**Test Suite**: `tests/test_no_lookahead.py` (9/9 Tests Passed)  
**Date**: September 16, 2026  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Lookahead bias is the single most common reason why algorithmic trading systems appear profitable in backtests but fail catastrophically in live markets. This audit established a rigorous automated testing framework to verify that no historical signal, indicator, or execution engine can access or react to future information.

---

## 2. Automated Test Methodologies

### 2.1. Future-Price Mutation Test
- **Methodology**: Historical price data was partitioned at bar $T = 120$. All future bars ($t > T$) were subjected to extreme random noise mutations (prices scaled $0.5\times$ to $2.5\times$, volume perturbed $0.1\times$ to $10\times$).
- **Requirement**: Signals generated on or before timestamp $T$ must remain 100% byte-for-byte identical.
- **Results**:
  - `ConfluenceGammaScalperStrategy`: **PASSED** (0 signal differences)
  - `GoldenTrendOptionBuyerStrategy`: **PASSED** (0 signal differences)
  - `ActiveMomentumOptionScalperStrategy`: **PASSED** (0 signal differences)
  - `LeaderBreakoutStrategy`: **PASSED** (0 signal differences)

### 2.2. History-Slice Causality Test
- **Methodology**: Strategies were evaluated at arbitrary points ($t = 60, 80, 100, 120, 140$) passing strictly the truncated slice `df[:t+1]`.
- **Requirement**: The signal at bar $t$ must match the signal generated by evaluating the full matrix `df[:]`.
- **Results**: **PASSED** across all strategies. No backward dependencies detected.

### 2.3. Execution Timing Test (Bar $T \rightarrow T+1$)
- **Methodology**: Verified that the event-driven `BacktestEngine` delays order execution until the bar *following* the signal.
- **Verification**: A signal generated at bar 25 close was confirmed to execute at bar 26 open. Same-bar close fills are strictly prohibited in the core engine.

### 2.4. Indicator Centering & Leakage Test
- **Methodology**: Tested all rolling calculations (EMAs, VWAP, Bollinger Bands, ATR, RSI) to confirm that `center=False` is enforced and no future forward-looking shifts (`shift(-k)`) exist in the feature pipelines.
- **Results**: **PASSED**.

## 3. Residual Intrabar Timing Warning

> [!WARNING]
> While the daily signal generation is temporally causal, **intraday simulation loops** in `confluence_scalper.py` and `golden_trend_buyer.py` evaluate $[Low, High]$ simultaneously against target and stop loss.  
> As detailed in `reports/CODE_AUDIT.md`, this does not constitute future leakage of tomorrow's bar, but introduces an **intrabar execution ordering bias**. This will be resolved in Phase 6 via the Conservative Intrabar Execution Engine.
